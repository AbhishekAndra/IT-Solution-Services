Master Services Agreement & Security Protocol

1. Confidentiality & Mutual Non-Disclosure

All architecture artifacts, source repository links, cloud cost telemetry, and performance benchmarks shared via the Stackly Client Portal are protected by strict bilateral non-disclosure provisions. Stackly employees hold active security clearances and adhere to SOC2 Type II and FedRAMP operational safeguards.

2. Client Tenant Isolation

Client telemetry workspaces are cryptographically partitioned using individual customer-managed encryption keys (CMEK). No cross-tenant data aggregation or unauthorized model fine-tuning is permitted on client codebases or operational telemetry.

3. SLA & Support Response Windows

Tier-1 Enterprise Engagements include guaranteed 15-minute response times for critical (P1) incident consultations and dedicated access to an assigned Principal Solutions Architect.

4. Intellectual Property & Deliverables

Upon signature and deliverable sign-off in the authenticated portal, all custom Terraform modules, Helm charts, CI/CD runbooks, and architecture specification blueprints transfer exclusively to the client enterprise.
